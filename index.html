Did you know?
The Japanese zodiac is called Jūnishi (十二支), meaning "Twelve Earthly Branches."

Each year is associated with one of 12 animals in a repeating cycle.

The broader Japanese term Eto (干支) can refer to the traditional 60-year cycle combining the 12 animals with the 5 elements.
